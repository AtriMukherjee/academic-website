Atri Mukherjee.

PhD researcher at SMIT (NUSE unit), Vrije Universiteit Brussel. I study how social class shapes people’s relationship to political news.

01Working on

  • Doctoral project within the ERC Starting Grant INEQNEWS: class as multidimensional, economic and cultural (institutionalised, objectified, embodied), and what it means for the political news people want, use, find and benefit from.
  • The view from down below: imagined journalists of economically disadvantaged audiences and journalism’s looming class disconnect
  • Towards an imagined audiences approach in studying structural inequalities in news useWith Kalogeropoulos, Gajardo, Picone, Rossini and Mont’Alverne. Work in progress.
  • Perceived social mobility and status enhancement through specific forms of news useEarly thinking.
