Research

Social class and political news

How economic and cultural class shape what people read, watch and listen to, and why.

Class is multidimensional

My doctoral research examines how social class shapes people’s relationship to political news. Drawing on Bourdieu’s theory of cultural capital, I treat class as more than income. I look at economic resources alongside three forms of cultural capital, and at how each is tied to the news people follow.

INEQNEWS

The project is part of INEQNEWS, an ERC Starting Grant project led by Prof. Dr. Antonis Kalogeropoulos. It aims to identify, theorise and reduce first-, second- and third-level digital inequalities in political news use, through comparative statistical analysis of people in different social positions across four media markets.
